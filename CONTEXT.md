# Polaris Context

Polaris is a local-first personal wayfinding workspace for turning larger goals into manageable work.

## Language

**Blueshift**:
A goal-oriented body of work that gathers related Stars. The name evokes moving toward a star and the resulting blueshift in its observed frequencies.
_Avoid_: Project, project

**Star**:
A single, actionable piece of work belonging to one Blueshift.
_Avoid_: Task, todo, to-do, item

**North Star**:
A Star designated as current focus. North Star is a persistent designation: there is no limit on how many North Stars may exist at once.
_Avoid_: Priority, featured task, daily task

### Atlas

**Atlas**:
The static site that documents the Polaris methodology: a Legend of concepts and Guides for working with them.
_Avoid_: docs site, cheat sheet site, help pages

**Legend**:
The canonical quick-reference of Polaris's concepts, wherever it appears.
_Avoid_: cheat sheet, glossary

**Guide**:
A walkthrough in the Atlas of one part of the Polaris methodology.
_Avoid_: chart, tutorial, how-to
